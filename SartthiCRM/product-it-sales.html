FAQ

Common questions.

Can I customize the sales pipeline stages?
+
Yes. You can create, rename, reorder, and delete pipeline stages to match your exact IT sales process. You can also set stage-specific automation rules.
How does the ticket-to-WhatsApp sync work?
+
Via Interakt integration, every support ticket can trigger a WhatsApp message to the client. Replies on WhatsApp appear in the CRM ticket thread automatically.
Is document storage secure?
+
All documents are stored on Cloudflare R2 with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS in transit. Access is controlled per-client with expiring share links.
