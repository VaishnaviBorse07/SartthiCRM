Our Story

Built in Ahmednagar,
built for India.

Most CRM companies are built in Silicon Valley for Silicon Valley companies. They don't understand GST compliance, WhatsApp-first communication, or the unique workflows of an Indian IT company, CA firm, or retail distributor.

We do. Because we are one. Sartthi AI was founded with a single conviction: Indian businesses deserve software that understands their context, their language, their regulatory environment — and their budget.

Today, SartthiCRM serves 500+ businesses across 50+ cities — from solo CAs in Nashik to IT companies in Pune and retail distributors in Mumbai.
