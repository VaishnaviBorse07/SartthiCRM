FAQ

Common questions.

Can I sync inventory with Tally or Marg ERP?
+
Yes. SartthiCRM has bi-directional sync with Tally ERP, TallyPrime, and Marg ERP. Stock levels, orders, and invoices sync automatically.
How many distributors can I manage?
+
There's no limit on the Growth or Enterprise plan. Each distributor gets a dedicated profile with their order history, credit limit, and outstanding balance.
Can customers check their order status on WhatsApp?
+
Yes. Via Interakt integration, customers can WhatsApp your business number to check their order status — the bot responds automatically with real-time CRM data.
